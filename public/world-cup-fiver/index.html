World Cup
Fiver5-A-SIDE · 32 NATIONS · STICK OR TWIST
  1. Pick a nation. Your squad comes from its own World Cup history, 1990 to 2026, plus the odd legend.
  2. Five players: one keeper, four outfield. Cards come up one at a time from a jumbled deck.
  3. Take him or twist. A twisted card is gone for this run, with no recall. If you run short of cards to finish your squad, the next one must be taken.
  4. Ratings stay hidden until the team sheet. Then pick a formation and a play style.
  5. Then the draw: 32 nations, 8 groups of 4. The top two in each group reach the round of 16, then it is knockout to the final, with extra time and penalties.
  6. Private test build. Progress is saved in this browser after every match.
