Rules
  • £20m each. You open the bidding on the first player, then you take it in turns to open.
  • Bids go up in £1m steps. Keep £1m back for every empty slot.
  • Each team needs exactly one goalkeeper. If the last keeper comes up and you have none, you must sign him.
  • Players out of position are penalised, some far more than others.
  • Your formation is picked automatically to suit your squad: 2-1-1, 1-2-1 or 1-1-2.
  • Once one squad is full, the other side gets each player for £1m, with one refusal.
  • You have 10 seconds to bid, or it counts as a pass.
  • Ratings stay hidden during the auction. You see your own when you pick your team.
